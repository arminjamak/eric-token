# Word meaning test

A new technical notation for describing systems reuses short English words as keywords.
For EACH keyword below, guess which ONE meaning (1-12) it most likely has, judging only by the word itself.
Several keywords may share a meaning. Give your confidence: 3 sure, 2 likely, 1 guessing.

## Meanings

1. one quantity is compared with another
2. a tag marking a feedback loop as self-amplifying (it escalates)
3. an accumulated amount that builds up or drains over time
4. an effect that arrives late, after a time delay
5. a flow that adds to an accumulated amount
6. a tag marking a feedback loop as goal-seeking: it settles at a target
7. a causal link: more of the first thing causes more of the second
8. movement or flow from one place to the next along a path
9. work divides into paths that run at the same time (in parallel)
10. a causal link: more of the first thing causes less of the second
11. a flow that drains an accumulated amount
12. flow returning to an earlier step (rework or repeat)
