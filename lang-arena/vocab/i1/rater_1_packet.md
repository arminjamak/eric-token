# Word meaning test

A new technical notation for describing systems reuses short English words as keywords.
For EACH keyword below, guess which ONE meaning (1-12) it most likely has, judging only by the word itself.
Several keywords may share a meaning. Give your confidence: 3 sure, 2 likely, 1 guessing.

## Meanings

1. branches that happen simultaneously, not one after another
2. things pass onward, step by step, in a direction
3. measure the gap between a value and a reference
4. label for a vicious or virtuous cycle that keeps feeding itself
5. an outgoing stream that empties a reservoir
6. an incoming stream that fills a reservoir
7. there is a waiting period before the consequence shows up
8. material goes in reverse, upstream to a previous stage
9. a reservoir-like quantity holding what has gathered so far
10. a cause-effect link where they move in opposite directions
11. label for a self-correcting cycle that stabilises toward a limit
12. a cause-effect link where both move in the same direction
